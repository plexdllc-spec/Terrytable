Six measures scaled against the rest of the league, where the outer edge is best. Compare any two teams, or one team against the league average.

All-play is your score against all 11 other teams each week. Expected wins is your all-play win rate times the weeks played. Luck is real wins minus expected wins: positive means the schedule helped you, negative means it hurt.

Consistency is the standard deviation of weekly scores. Lower means steadier. Sorted by average score.
